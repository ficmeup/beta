Voices

Piper voices sound natural, work on phones and computers, and keep playing with the screen locked. Each voice downloads about 60 MB the first time you pick it. Linda and Cori are trained on audiobook narration.

Kokoro voices (computers only) are the most natural. They download about 310 MB once and need a recent browser.

Built-in voices need no download, but stop when the screen locks.

Safari only lets websites use a basic set of built-in voices. Voices you download in your device’s Accessibility settings (Premium, Enhanced, Siri) don’t appear here; that’s Apple’s rule for websites.

Adding this app to your Home Screen keeps downloaded voices from being cleared.
