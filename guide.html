Fic Me Up reads AO3 works aloud, in your browser, on your own device.

This page covers opening it, adding a story, choosing a voice and what it can’t do. Reading it takes about four minutes.

Open Fic Me Up
Cost
Free. No account, no sign-up.
Your library
Stays on the device you add it on. Nothing is uploaded.
AO3
The app makes no requests to AO3. You download the work, or it reads the page you already have open.

01 · Put it on your Home Screen

Use Fic Me Up as an app, and add all your fics to it.

  • iPhone and iPad. Open ficmeup.github.io in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new icon from now on.
  • Android. Open it in Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen.
  • Computer. A bookmark is enough.

On iPhone, the Home Screen app and Safari keep separate libraries. Stick to the icon, and every fic you add stays in one place, with its voice downloaded once.

02 · Add a fic

Find a fic on AO3 ↗, next to Paste in the app, opens AO3 in your browser. Then add the fic one of five ways. The first three need no setup.

  1. Download it from AO3. On the work, tap Download → EPUB. In Fic Me Up, tap Open a file and choose it from Downloads. Chapters, titles and Work info come through.
  2. Copy the text, from any website. In Safari, tap aA → Show Reader (on AO3, tap Entire Work first if it has chapters). Press and hold on the text, tap Select All, then Copy. In Fic Me Up, tap Paste. The first line becomes the title.
  3. A book or file from another app. In Books: Library → ⋯ on the book → Share → Save to Files. In Files, Mail and other apps: Share → Save to Files. Then in Fic Me Up, tap Open a file. EPUB, PDF, Word (.docx), HTML and text work. Copying a book and pasting it doesn’t work: iPhones let websites paste text, not files.
  4. Faster, after a one-time setup: Shortcuts. Two small Apple Shortcuts put Add to Fic Me Up and Send file to Fic Me Up in the Share button, so a page or file goes to the app in two taps, then Paste. How to make them is below.
  5. On a computer: the Listen bookmark. Set it up from The Listen bookmark for AO3 on the app’s main page. It works on other websites too.

None of these fetch anything from AO3; they use the page or file you already have. A copied link on its own can’t be opened: the app would have to download the page, and browsers don’t allow that.

Books bought in Apple Books are copy-protected, and Apple doesn’t let any app open them; their Share button offers a Book Store link instead of Save to Files. Books you added yourself work.

Set up Add to Fic Me Up (iPhone, once)

For way 4. A Shortcut is made once in Apple’s Shortcuts app; after that it appears when you tap Share in Safari. If someone sent you a link to it, open the link and tap Add Shortcut, then do step 1 only. To make it yourself:

  1. Settings → Apps → Shortcuts → Advanced → turn on Allow Running Scripts.
  2. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +.
  3. Add the action Run JavaScript on Web Page. Delete the code in it and paste this: 
    var r = document.querySelector('#workskin');
var m = document.querySelector('dl.work.meta');
var html = r ? (m ? m.outerHTML : '') + r.outerHTML : document.documentElement.outerHTML;
completion('FICMEUP1:' + JSON.stringify({ html: html, url: location.href.split('#')[0] }));
  4. Add the action Copy to Clipboard.
  5. Add the action Show Notification with the text: Copied. Open Fic Me Up and tap Paste.
  6. Tap the name at the top, call it Add to Fic Me Up. Open its settings (ⓘ), turn on Show in Share Sheet, and set it to accept Safari web pages only.

To use it: in Safari, tap Share and scroll past the row of apps; Add to Fic Me Up is in the list below. (Edit Actions… at the bottom of that list can move it to the top.) The first time on a website, iPhone asks whether it may run there: tap Allow.

Set up Send file to Fic Me Up (iPhone, once)

For way 4, files. After it’s made, it appears when you tap Share on a file in Files, Mail, Books and other apps.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +.
  2. Add the action Base64 Encode. Tap its arrow (›) and set Line Breaks to None.
  3. Add the action Text. Type FICMEUPF1:, then insert the variable Shortcut Input, tap it and choose Name, type ., insert Shortcut Input again and choose File Extension, type :, then insert Base64 Encoded. No spaces anywhere.
  4. Add the action Copy to Clipboard.
  5. Add the action Show Notification with the text: Copied. Open Fic Me Up and tap Paste.
  6. Call it Send file to Fic Me Up. In its settings (ⓘ), turn on Show in Share Sheet and set it to accept Files and PDFs.

AO3’s Rating-to-Stats block shows above the fic under Work info and isn’t read aloud. Chapter titles are.

03 · Choose a voice

Tap the line under the play button to open speed, voice and sleep timer. Voices that need a download have a Preview first; nothing downloads until you tap Download.

Piper
12 voices. 60 MB each, downloaded once. Phones and computers. Keeps reading with the screen locked. Linda and Cori are trained on audiobook narration.
Kokoro
12 voices. 310 MB, downloaded once. Computers only; too heavy for a phone browser.
Built-in
No download. Stops when the screen locks.

Use Wi-Fi for the first download.

04 · Listening

  • Play once. It reads to the end of the work, chapter to chapter.
  • Tap any sentence to start from there. ⟲30 and 30⟳ skip 30 seconds; the arrows move one sentence.
  • Sleep timer: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end of the chapter. It stops between sentences, never mid-way.
  • Speed runs from 0.5× to 2×. Each story keeps its own.
  • Your place is saved. Open the story again and it picks up at the same sentence.
  • With a Piper voice, play, pause and skip also work from the lock screen.
  • Ten seconds after you press play, the player folds down to one row. Tap it to open it again.
  • Text size (15 to 32 px) and font are in the same panel as speed.
  • The chapter list stays at the top while you scroll. Pick a chapter to go straight to it. AO3 ↗ beside it opens the fic’s AO3 page.
  • The ⌄ button on the player folds it down or opens it again.
  • AO3’s Rating-to-Stats block shows above the story under Work info. It isn’t read aloud.
  • Pronunciations, on the main page: for names the voice gets wrong. Write the word, then how it sounds in plain letters (Hermione → her my oh nee), tap Test, then Add. There’s also a shared list everyone gets; yours wins if both have the same word. The text on screen never changes.

05 · Queue, playlists, notes

  • ⋯ on a fic: Play next, Add to queue, add it to a playlist or start a new one, or Share its AO3 link.
  • When a story ends, the next one in the queue starts after 8 seconds. Stay here stops it.
  • A playlist’s Play all plays it in order. Share sends it as a link: titles, authors and AO3 addresses only, never the text. Whoever opens it gets the playlist, with links to AO3 for any fic they haven’t added yet.
  • Your notes, above each fic, save as you type and are never read aloud. The Notes tab in the library lists every fic you’ve written notes on.
  • At the end of a story: Leave kudos on AO3. There is no rating inside the app; kudos and comments on AO3 are what the writer sees.

06 · What it can’t do

  • Safari lets websites use a basic set of built-in voices only. Premium, Enhanced and Siri voices you’ve downloaded in Settings don’t appear.
  • Kokoro doesn’t run on iPhone or iPad. The full model runs out of memory; the smaller one takes 2.5 seconds to make each second of speech.
  • Built-in voices stop when the screen locks. Piper voices don’t.
  • A story added on your phone isn’t on your computer, and on iPhone the Home Screen app and Safari don’t share a library. Queue, playlists and notes stay on the device too, until sign-in arrives.
  • Fic Me Up isn’t made by or connected to AO3 or the Organization for Transformative Works.
Managing the shared pronunciations (app owner only)
  1. On GitHub, signed in: Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens → Generate new token.
  2. Name: ficmeup-pronunciations. Resource owner: ficmeup. Expiration: 1 year.
  3. Repository access: Only select repositories → ficmeup/data.
  4. Permissions → Repository permissions → Contents: Read and write. Nothing else.
  5. Generate token and copy it. If GitHub says the organization must approve it, approve it under the ficmeup organization’s Settings → Personal access tokens → Pending requests.
  6. In Fic Me Up: Pronunciations → I manage the shared list, and paste the token. It stays on that device.

After that, the Add to menu offers the shared list, and shared entries get Remove. Changes reach everyone within about a minute. The token can only change that one list.

07 · The writers

The writer gets nothing from this app: no hits, no kudos, no comments. If you listen to something here, go back to AO3 and leave kudos or a comment.

The audio is for your own listening. Don’t record it or share it; that would be an unauthorised podfic. Some writers say in their profile or notes that they don’t want their work read by text-to-speech. Respect that.

08 · Problems

If a voice stops or won’t start, tap the status line under the player. A log opens; send a screenshot of it with a line on what you were doing to github.com/ficmeup/ficmeup.github.io/issues, or to whoever shared this link with you.
