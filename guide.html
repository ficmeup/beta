HOW TO USE FIC ME UP

Open Fic Me Up
Cost
Free. No account, no sign-up.
Your library
Stays on the device you add it on. Nothing is uploaded.
AO3
The app makes no requests to AO3. You download the work, or it reads the page you already have open.
Works on
iPhone, iPad, Android and computers, in the browser.

01 · Put it on your Home Screen

Recommended

Use Fic Me Up as an app, and add all your fics to it.

  • iPhone and iPad. Open ficmeup.github.io in your browser, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new Home Screen icon from now on.
  • Android. Open it in Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen.
  • Computer. A bookmark is enough.

Tip: On iPhone, the Home Screen app and Safari keep separate libraries. Stick to the Home Screen app and every fic you add stays in one place, with its voice downloaded once, as browsers may clear your data regularly and you could lose your fics.

02 · Add a fic

You can use Fic Me Up directly from your browser, your bookmarks, or the Home Screen app.

  1. Download a fic from AO3. On the fic, tap Entire Work, then Download → EPUB. TapOpen a downloaded file. Tap Open a file, find the fic and go your merry way. :)
  2. Similarly, other books from your collection can be listened to through Fic Me Up (it won’t work on books bought through Books, Kindle and so on). You know what, you’re allowed to listen to your school or work docs too.
  3. Copy the text, from any website. In Safari, tap aA → Show Reader (on AO3, tap Entire Work first if it has chapters). Press and hold on the text, tap Select All, then Copy. In Fic Me Up, tap Paste.
  4. On a computer. Use the Listen bookmark.
  5. On iPhones.
    2. Open any web page → Share → Add Bookmark. Name the bookmark Listen and save it to the folder Favorites.
    3. Go to Bookmarks → Favorites and Edit the Listen bookmark. Replace the address with the code copied above. Tap Done.
    4. Open AO3 in your browser → tap Entire Work → Open the page in your browser → tap ⋯ → click Bookmarks → Listen. It opens Fic Me Up, and you can listen away. On a phone, choose one of the two options: Open in browser, or Copy for the Home Screen app.
  6. Home Screen app. Once you’ve added a fic from the Home Screen app, it should work exactly like in a browser.

03 · Choose a voice

Tap the line under the play button to open speed, voice and sleep timer. Voices that need a download have a Preview first; nothing downloads until you tap Download.

Piper
12 voices. 60 MB each, downloaded once. Phones and computers. Keeps reading with the screen locked. Linda and Cori are trained on audiobook narration.
Kokoro
12 voices. 310 MB, downloaded once. Computers only; too heavy for a phone browser.
Built-in
No download. Stops when the screen locks.

Use Wi-Fi for the first download.

Tip: Once you start playing a fic, stay on the screen, unlocked, for a minute or two for a lag-free experience.

04 · Listening

  • Play once. It reads to the end of the work, chapter to chapter.
  • Tap any sentence to start from there. ⟲30 and 30⟳ skip 30 seconds; the arrows move one sentence.
  • Sleep timer: 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or the end of the chapter.
  • Speed runs from 0.5× to 2×. Each storydocument keeps its own memory.
  • Your place is saved. Open the storydocument again and it picks up at the same sentence.
  • With a Piper voice, play, pause and skip also work from the lock screen.
  • The chapter list stays at the top while you scroll. Pick a chapter to go straight to it. AO3 ↗ beside it opens the fic’s AO3 page.

05 · Queue, playlists, notes

  • ⋯ on a fic: Play next, Add to queue, add it to a playlist or start a new one, or Share its AO3 link.
  • When a storydocument ends, the next one in the queue starts after 8 seconds. Stay here stops it.
  • A playlist’s Play all plays it in order. Share sends it as an AO3a link. Whoever opens it gets the playlist, with links to AO3 for any fic they haven’t added yet.
  • Your notes, above each fic, save as you type. The Notes tab in the library lists every fic you’ve written notes on.
  • At the end of a story: Leave kudos on AO3. There is no rating inside the app; kudos and comments on AO3 are what the writer sees.

06 · What it can’t do

  • Safari lets websites use a basic set of built-in voices only. Premium, Enhanced and Siri voices you’ve downloaded in Settings don’t appear.
  • The app may not work properly on older phones, and it may drain the battery faster on them. I’m trying to sort this out, but there’s only so much I can do.
  • A storydocument added on your phone isn’t on your computer, and on iPhone the Home Screen app and Safari don’t share a library. Queue, playlists and notes stay on the device too, until sign-in arrives, which I’m working on.
  • If your battery is low or your phone is warming up, please wait till the battery is charged and the phone has cooled down to use the app.
  • Fic Me Up isn’t made by or connected to AO3 or the Organization for Transformative Works.

08 · The writers

The writer gets nothing from this app: no hits, no kudos, no comments. If you listen to something here, go back to AO3where you found it and leave kudos or a comment.

The audio is for your own listening. Don’t record it or share it; that would be an unauthorised podfic. Some writers say in their profile or notes that they don’t want their work read by text-to-speech. Respect that.

09 · Problems

If a voice stops or won’t start, tap the status line under the player. A log opens; send a screenshot of it with a line on what you were doing to github.com/ficmeup/ficmeup.github.io/issues, or to whoever shared this link with you. You can also get in touch with me through the Ko-fi link.

I’m doing something like this for the first time, so please be kind.
